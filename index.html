How it works
  1. Choose a Plan
    • Select Starter, Standard or Agent Pack, and pay.
    • You will receive your Emailed Stripe Receipt Number and an order number.
  2. Send Your Photos
    • Take or choose photos on your device to upload.
    • Your photos will show in the order you choose.
    • Once you are happy with your selection, tap Send.
    • You will receive a private code. Use this code to collect your video when it is ready.
  3. Get Your Video
    • Open the Get Your Video page.
    • Enter your order number and private code, then tap Find My Order.
    • When your video is ready, tap Download.

Questions
Do the photos actually move?

The camera gently zooms and glides across each photo, with soft fades between them. The photos themselves stay still. It is a smooth moving slideshow, saved as an MP4 video.

How long is each video?
  • Every pack offers a choice of 30 seconds, 60 seconds or 1 minute 30 seconds.
  • In Motion Studio, you can choose your own length.
What will I get?

An MP4 video in 1080p, in the shape you choose: wide, tall for Reels, post size or square.

How do I send my photos?
  • Choose your package and pay. A short form will open with your Emailed Stripe Receipt Number.
  • Upload photos or take new ones.
  • Choose your video length and place your photos in order.
  • Write a short message and choose whether you would like music.
  • Tap Send when you are finished.
  • Keep your receipt number, as you will use it each time you send photos.
How do I use my credits on another device?
  • Open Motion Studio on the new device.
  • Tap Buy Credits at the top. You will not be charged.
  • Tap Restore My Credits and enter your Emailed Stripe Receipt Number, for example #1234-5678.
  • Your credits are ready to use.
Is the music OK to use?

Yes. We only use royalty-free music, so you can post your video anywhere.

Prefer to do it yourself?

Try the Motion Studio app on your device. Make free videos with the small fionah icon, as many as you like. Buy credits for videos without it.
