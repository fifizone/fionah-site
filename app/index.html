💡 Tips for the best results

  • Your phone opens its own picker for photos and music. If it asks, tap Allow Full Access so you can pick lots at once.
  • Songs from Apple Music or Spotify are locked and can't be used. Use a song saved on your phone, in Files or in Drive.
  • Bold looks best at 3–6 seconds per photo. Longer times zoom in a lot and photos can look soft.
  • While making the video, keep this screen open and switched on until it finishes.
  • Bright daylight photos with tidy rooms look best.
